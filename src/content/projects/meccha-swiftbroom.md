## A new hiding place in Swiftbroom

This is my Wizard map for **Meccha Chameleon**, based on Swiftbroom Academy from R.E.P.O. It is published as **R.E.P.O - Swiftbroom** on Steam Workshop.

## Development

This was my first map. My work includes the Meccha Chameleon adaptation, additional hiding props, lighting adjustments, and Workshop updates. The original R.E.P.O. setting and its assets belong to their creators.
